## Themes and Patterns This Week

### 1. AI ROI measurement is the dominant McKinsey theme
Three McKinsey pieces this week converge on the same signal: companies are spending on AI but failing to convert it to measurable outcomes. "More spending, elusive impact" (May 4), "From promise to impact" (Apr 24), and "Rewiring for AI: From Ambition to Advantage" (May 7) all frame AI value capture—not AI deployment—as the unsolved enterprise problem. This is the most consistent signal cluster of the week.

### 2. Pharma digital marketing compliance is under active FDA enforcement
Alnylam's FDA rebuke for misleading open-label data on a consumer website (May 7) arrives in a week where FDA transparency norms are also questioned (blocked vaccine safety studies, May 6). Pharma marketing teams need to treat digital channel compliance as a live enforcement risk, not a background consideration.

### 3. Pre-launch pharma marketing has measurable long-term ROI
ZoomRx analysis (FiercePharma, May 8) providing quantified evidence that pre-launch HCP awareness creates durable competitive advantage is the strongest evidence-backed pharma marketing insight this week. It directly supports the case for sustained pre-launch investment in pharma brand building.

### 4. Peak Martech as a structural signal
ChiefMartec's 15,505-product landscape with near-zero growth (0.79%) signals market saturation with high underlying churn. The consolidation signal is important for any marketing operations leader making stack rationalization decisions.

### 5. Agentic AI is moving into business model language
Microsoft using "agentic business model" in earnings communications, OpenAI-AWS formalizing managed agent infrastructure (Bedrock Managed Agents), and McKinsey publishing infrastructure guidance for agentic AI all indicate that agentic AI has crossed from experimental to investment-grade business architecture.

### 6. Retail Dive and Food Dive both returned 503 errors
Both feeds have been reliable in prior weeks. Simultaneous 503 errors suggest possible CDN or rate-limiting issue on the run date. No consumer/retail content captured from these sources this week.

### 7. CMO Confidential and Marketing AI Institute remain stale
CMO Confidential: newest article from December 2025. Marketing AI Institute: newest qualifying article from April 2026 but conference-preview format, not substantive analysis. Both feeds are trending toward removal from active list.
